# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static site, no backend: Vite + TypeScript (from the brief). Framework delegated to the build — chosen at the start of development. Hosting: GitHub Pages at `https://alexandrbaryshov.github.io/diva_nails/`, published only on the owner's word.

## Users

Women first — clients of a nail studio in Cheboksary who open the site from a phone (VK, Yandex Maps, stories) and want to show the master exactly what they want. Men second, in two roles: as clients (neat hands without "salon" feel, men's manicure without coating, podology) and as gift-givers (a gift certificate for her).

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for Diva Nails whose first screen is a manicure constructor: the visitor taps a nail, paints a design, saves a 9:16 brief card to the phone gallery and books (Telegram or YCLIENTS), then shows the card to the master. Success: open → tap a nail → save the card → book.

## Positioning

The only studio in town where you arrive with your own design already drawn on a hand, in the studio's real gel-polish palette, with shade codes the master finds on the shelf at once. The constructor has no separate name — it is "the manicure constructor" inside the Diva Nails brand.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first: almost every visit is a phone, one-handed, often from a social-network link. The result travels as a PNG in the phone gallery, as a Telegram message with a design link, and as a QR on the card the master scans at the appointment. Everything runs in the browser; uploaded pictures never leave the device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Constructor: per-nail colour, coating effects (gloss, matte, chrome, rub-in, cat-eye, glitter, ombré), ready designs (French, lunar, ombré, marble, dots, stripes, hearts), draggable decor (rhinestones, stickers), six shapes and lengths up to extra, own picture as a nail print or as a reference, undo/redo/reset, her/his hand, 3–4 skin tones, second hand.
- Design state lives in the URL hash; no server.
- Russian only. Buttons ≥ 44 px, text labels on colours, keyboard on desktop, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
- Undecided, waiting for the studio (shown as «уточняется»): logo file, price list, real gel-polish palette, photos of works / studio / masters, hours, second phone, how to find the entrance, FAQ answers, gift-certificate terms, YCLIENTS link, year founded, Metrica counter.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Diva Nails. The studio has a logo; it must be used as is once the file arrives — until then a wordmark stand-in.
- Pinned mood from the brief: «Молочный нюд» — milky, powdery, warm beige, airy, soft shadows, "expensive and calm"; bright colour appears only on the nails in the constructor. Starting palette from the brief (to be checked against the logo): milk `#F6F0EA`, cream `#FBF8F4`, powder pink `#E7CFC4`, caramel nude `#C9A48E`, text warm dark brown `#3B2A26`, secondary cocoa `#7A645C`.
- Type: headings in a thin elegant face with character (display serif or contrast grotesque) with good Cyrillic; body in a calm readable grotesque.
- Anchor phrase from a review: «всё продумано до мелочей».
- Composition chosen by the owner (2026-10-24): the category standard played straight — the constructor as a calm photo-editor-style screen — with the filled warm-skin vector hand from the "tip fan" mock. No external comparables were named; the approved comp is the bar.
- Voice: «на ты», like a friend who is a nail master — warm, simple, light humour, no pomp; for men the same tone but calmer, no «ноготочки» and hearts.

## Product Principles

1. The constructor is the front door: a nail is tappable in the first second, no scroll.
2. Only what the studio can really do: colours, effects and decor come from the studio's stock, labelled by brand and shade number.
3. The card is a brief for the master: everything needed to reproduce the design is on it, readable at a glance.
4. Calm, considered, "всё продумано до мелочей" — the site recedes so the polish is the hero.
5. Nothing about the studio is invented; what is missing is visibly «уточняется».

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch targets ≥ 44 px; every colour carries a text label (brand + number), not only a swatch; full keyboard path on desktop; `prefers-reduced-motion` replaces brush strokes and the lamp sequence with instant changes.
